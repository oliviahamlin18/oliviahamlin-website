Understand your neurotype

Autism assessments for adults and young people, carried out by a clinical psychologist and paced to suit you.

Make an enquiry

Many people spend years wondering why things that look easy for others take them so much effort. An assessment can put a name to that experience and point towards support that fits.

Who I assess

Adults

For adults who think they may be autistic, whether the idea is new or has been there since school. We look at your developmental history alongside how work and relationships feel now.

Young people

For young people and the families around them. Appointments are paced to suit the young person, and parents or carers contribute to the developmental history.

How an assessment works

Four stages, so you know what comes next at every point.

  1. Get in touch

    Send a short email. We arrange a brief call to check that an assessment is the right next step.

  2. Share some background

    You complete questionnaires and a developmental history before your appointment, which leaves session time free for conversation.

  3. Assessment appointments

    Structured conversation and observation using recognised tools such as the ADOS-2 and ADI-R.

  4. Feedback and report

    A feedback session to talk through the findings, then a written report you can use to access support.
